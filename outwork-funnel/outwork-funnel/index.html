      The mechanism

      How ad spend becomes customers,
      not impressions.

      Most video agencies make brand content and hope it converts later. We build direct response upfront, so every ad is engineered to get one specific person to raise their hand.

      Four principles behind every script we write.

      1. Call out the right person. The ad should make your buyer think "this is for me" in the first two seconds, using a specific problem, situation or outcome.

      2. Lead with what they want. Customers care about more customers, more revenue and less hassle. Your service is just the mechanism that gets them there.

      3. Give them a reason to believe. Proof, results, testimonials, experience, a clear process. Attention on its own isn't enough.

      4. Make the next step obvious. Get a quote. Book a call. Request an assessment. If the viewer doesn't know what to do next, the ad hasn't finished its job.

      Frequently asked

      The short answers.

      Who is this actually for?
      Service businesses turning over £20k a month or more, with a sales process already in place, who want a predictable flow of qualified enquiries into it rather than relying on referrals and word of mouth. If you're earlier stage than that, say so on the form. We run a done-with-you consultancy that fits better and costs a lot less.
      What exactly do I get?
      30 full-funnel video creatives, one full production day on location, all the strategy, messaging and scripting that sits behind them, three months of Meta ads management, the lead generation system itself (ads, lead forms, targeting, pre-qualification) and monthly lead reporting.
      Why 30 videos and not one really good one?
      Because we don't want to bet your entire ad spend on a single idea. Different customers respond to different things: the problem, the outcome, the offer, proof, education. Thirty videos give Meta something to actually test, so real customer behaviour tells us what works instead of us guessing.
      How much should I budget for ad spend?
      We recommend £6,000 across 90 days, which typically equates to your ads being shown 500,000+ times. The practical minimum to get clean data is £1,000 a month. Ad spend is paid directly to Meta and sits separately from our fee.
      How is it priced?
      £3,500 per month with a three-month minimum, which is £10,500 for the full 90-day engine, including the filming day and all 30 creatives.
      Why a 3-month minimum?
      Because the system needs a full test-and-scale cycle to work. Month one is launch and hook testing, month two is identifying winners and cutting losers, month three is scaling spend behind what's proven. Stopping at 30 days means paying for the learning and none of the return.
      Does this replace my current marketing person?
      No. We become your video ad production and Meta ads team. It plugs into your existing setup. CRM integration, sales team alignment and lead handling process are part of the build.
      What happens on the filming day?
      Eight structured hours at your business or a location of your choice, with a director guiding delivery and a videographer filming. Everything is scripted and approved beforehand, so the day is about capturing multiple hooks and variations, plus b-roll of your service actually being carried out.
